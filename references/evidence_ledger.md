# Evidence ledger

This ledger constrains how each source may be used. It is intentionally shorter than a literature review: a source is not cited for claims beyond its stated scope.

| Claim ID | Label | Permitted statement | Source | Scope / caveat |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| E1 | FACT — cited | TPT-Bench contains 48 robot-egocentric target-person sequences collected in crowded/unstructured indoor/outdoor settings, with long occlusion and re-identification challenges. | [R1](references.md#r1) | The data collection used a pushed sensor cart; no autonomous-robot success claim follows. |
| E2 | FACT — cited | The public TPT record reports 5.3 h of recordings and 571,982 target-person boxes. | [R2](references.md#r2) | Copyright is listed in the record; do not re-host data. |
| E3 | FACT — cited | VISTA uses synchronized first- and third-person video to separate viewpoint effects from activity-domain effects. | [R3](references.md#r3) | It is not a robot-person-tracking benchmark. |
| E4 | FACT — cited | EgoTracks is a long-term egocentric tracking resource with per-frame location and presence confidence. | [R4](references.md#r4) | Respect Ego4D access conditions; do not turn it into person/robot evidence. |
| E5 | FACT — cited | TrackVLA represents a broader embodied-tracking direction that joins recognition and trajectory planning. | [R5](references.md#r5) | Not an embedded Nano baseline. |
| E6 | FACT — cited | VOT-LT2022 requires disappearance handling, re-detection, and confidence reporting. | [R6](references.md#r6) | Generic long-term tracking only. |
| E7 | FACT — cited | FARTrack is an ICLR 2026 autoregressive tracker built around Task-Specific Self-Distillation, Inter-frame Autoregressive Sparsification, multi-template appearance modeling, and autoregressive trajectory prediction. | [R11](references.md#r11) | This establishes the published method, not identity-safe target-person tracking. |
| E8 | FACT — cited | FARTrack reports Tiny/Nano/Pico at 6.82M/4.59M/2.81M parameters and 2.65G/1.78G/1.08G MACs, with speed measured on Titan Xp, Xeon Gold 6230R, and Ascend 310B; training used 8× RTX A6000. | [R11](references.md#r11) | No Jetson Nano FPS follows from these figures. |
| E9 | AUTHOR-REPORTED LIMITATION | FARTrack reports that prolonged tracking failure such as disappearance/occlusion can make maintained templates invalid and degrade tracking performance. | [R11](references.md#r11) | This motivates but does not itself prove an identity-corruption mechanism. |
| E10 | CODE-SOURCE AVAILABLE | The official FARTrack repository releases implementation/checkpoint/configuration material for reproduction and implementation-level auditing. | [R12](references.md#r12) | Any behavioral claim from code must be recorded separately as CODE FACT after inspecting the relevant path/version. |
| I1 | INTERPRETATION — reasoned | Identity continuity after disappearance and similar-person distraction is the right immediate perception problem for this project. | E1–E6 | A project framing, not a publication result or social-impact ranking. |
| I2 | INTERPRETATION — reasoned | FARTrack is attractive as a development backbone because efficiency is already a first-class design goal while its reported limitation overlaps the chosen disappearance/recovery failure regime. | E7–E10 | Does not establish that FARTrack is the best possible baseline. |
| H1 | HYPOTHESIS TO TEST | A wrong FARTrack prediction may jointly contaminate spatial state, trajectory history, and appearance-template history, creating an autoregressive self-confirmation loop. | E9–E10 | Must be tested by instrumented code and failure episodes before being stated as a result. |
| H2 | HYPOTHESIS TO TEST | IFAS localization-oriented token importance may discard cues that are weak for localization but strong for person identity discrimination. | E7, E10 | Requires token-level/identity experiments; not an author claim. |
| D1 | PROJECT DECISION — provisional | Use a consent-based guide/accompany scenario as the current application framing. | I1 | Must be reopened if the task, data, sensor, or field partner changes. |
| D2 | PROJECT DECISION — provisional | Treat TPT-Bench as domain validation and keep detector-assisted modes separate from pure SOT. | E1–E2 | Final protocol must be copied from the current official toolkit before experiments. |
| D3 | PROJECT DECISION — provisional | Use FARTrack as the current tracker family; FARTrack-Nano is the main development candidate, Tiny is the higher-capacity reference, and Pico is the later edge candidate. | I2 | Reversible after reproduction, failure audit, or hardware profiling. |
